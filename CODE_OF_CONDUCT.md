# Code of Conduct

We want DataMind to be a project where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or send a fix without feeling foolish or unwelcome. That goes for issues, pull requests, reviews and any other place where we talk about the project.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient. Everyone was new to SQL, Snowflake or open source once.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume someone is wrong.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This query scans the whole table" helps. "This is lazy" doesn't.
- Accept that people will disagree with you, and that that's fine.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Don't share anyone's personal details without their permission.

## What isn't acceptable
- Harassment, insults or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or any unwelcome sexual attention.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or trolling.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an email address, a key or a home address.

## If something goes wrong
If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately. Use the contact options on the maintainers' GitHub profiles, or open an issue asking for a private contact without including any details. We'll read every report, keep it confidential, and respond as fairly as we can. Depending on what happened, that can range from a quiet word to removing someone from the project.

## Scope
This applies to everyone involved in the project, maintainers included, in project spaces and wherever someone is representing the project.
